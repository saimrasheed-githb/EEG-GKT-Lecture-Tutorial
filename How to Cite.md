## Please also cite the original data

The real-data slides and all notebooks use the public dataset of Gao et al. When using the notebooks or results, please also cite the original article and the dataset:

**Article**
Gao, J., Tian, H., Yang, Y., Yu, X., Li, C., & Rao, N. (2014). A novel algorithm to enhance P300 in single trials: Application to lie detection using F-score and SVM. *PLOS ONE, 9*(11), e109700. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0109700

Gao, J., Tian, H., Yang, Y., Yu, X., Li, C., & Rao, N. (2015). *Data from: A novel algorithm to enhance P300 in single trials: Application to lie detection using F-score and SVM* [Dataset]. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.2qc64
